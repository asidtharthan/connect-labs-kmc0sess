What's computed and what isn't?

10 flags compute from real production data: Low Visits/Case, Low Mortality, High Mortality, Late Enrollment, High Danger Signs, Zero Danger Signs, Weight Loss, Weight Gain, Zero Weight Change, Rounded Weights.

6 flags show "—" because their underlying form fields aren't yet wired: Copy-Paste HR (heart_rate), Copy-Paste Temp (temperature), Implausible SpO2 (spo2_level), Gestational-Age Full-Term (gestational_age_lmp), GPS Spread (gps_lat/lon), DS Without Referral (child_referred). These will populate once form paths are discovered via the CommCare MCP server.

Methodology: Same thresholds and rules as the existing kmc_flw_flags workflow template. Cross-checked against the Overview of KMC flags [March 2026] doc. 27 unit tests cover threshold boundaries.

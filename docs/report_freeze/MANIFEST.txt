PRIVACY: WORKER IDS ARE RE-KEYED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every worker identifier in these files is FLW_xxxxxx, a salted SHA-256 of the Connect id truncated
to 6 hex. No Connect id, session UUID, name, phone, LGA or settlement appears anywhere. Verified: 0
20-hex ids and 0 session UUIDs across all five files. This repo is a PUBLIC fork and the refresh
workflow states no participant data goes in git, which is why the re-keying exists.

Every figure remains fully recomputable, because the codes are only labels:
  panel_gaps -> retention 328 of 367 = 89.4%
  2wt_lags   -> 467 of 484 completers within 2 days, 467/515 started = 90.7%
  payload    -> PANEL 3,491 | ABT2 1,201 | ABT2-A 92.1% | Overall 9,431

To re-link internally you need BOTH of these, and both are gitignored:
  .report_freeze_salt                     the 256-bit salt
  docs/report_freeze/.id_map_v207.csv     code -> connect_id, 1,478 rows
Losing the salt makes the mapping unreproducible. That is the intended property, so keep a copy
somewhere durable if internal re-linking matters later.

The session_id column was DROPPED from 2wt_lags rather than hashed: it identifies a conversation
and contributes nothing to auditing a lag.
